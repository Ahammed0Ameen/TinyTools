FAQ

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is compressed by your browser using a canvas, and it is not sent to a server by this tool.

Why is PNG not an option?

Browsers only apply quality settings to JPEG and WebP. Transparent areas in PNG files become white in JPEG output; WebP keeps transparency.
